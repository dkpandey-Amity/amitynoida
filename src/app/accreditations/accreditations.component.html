Top Global Accreditations

Amity University Noida is a UGC-recognised, not-for-profit private university committed to maintaining high standards of academic quality, governance, and institutional integrity. Its accreditations and recognitions reflect sustained adherence to national and international benchmarks in higher education.

Amity University is Asia’s only university to be awarded the highest level of accreditation by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges (WASC), USA, and by the Quality Assurance Agency for Higher Education (QAA), United Kingdom. These globally respected accreditation bodies assess institutions on parameters such as academic governance, teaching–learning processes, research standards, student support systems, and continuous improvement mechanisms.

The accreditation by WASC and QAA signifies that Amity University Noida meets internationally accepted standards of academic quality and institutional effectiveness. These validations underscore the university’s structured approach to curriculum design, research-led teaching, faculty development, and student engagement.

At the national level, Amity University Noida complies with regulatory frameworks laid down by statutory bodies and continuously works to strengthen its academic delivery, research capability, and quality assurance processes. Accreditation outcomes are integrated into institutional planning to ensure consistency, accountability, and long-term academic improvement.

These recognitions enable Amity University Noida to collaborate with leading global institutions, support academic mobility, and offer degrees that are recognised and respected internationally. They also provide assurance to students, parents, and industry partners regarding the credibility, transparency, and academic rigour of the university.

Through continuous evaluation and alignment with global best practices, Amity University Noida remains committed to advancing quality education, fostering research excellence, and creating an academic environment that supports learning, innovation, and societal contribution.
