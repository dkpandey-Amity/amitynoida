International Students at Amity University Noida

Ever since its inception, international students have been an integral part of the Amity University Noida campus community. Students from countries across the world choose Amity as their preferred destination to study in India, attracted by its strong academic ecosystem, global outlook, and student-centric support systems. For many foreign students, Amity becomes more than a place of learning; it truly feels like a home away from home.

With a strong national presence and a growing global footprint, Amity University, Noida, is recognised for delivering a globally aligned higher education experience. Located in the Delhi NCR region, the University attracts students from across India and overseas, creating a culturally diverse and academically rich campus environment.

Through internationally benchmarked curricula, cross-cultural exposure, and a strong focus on holistic development, Amity University Noida remains committed to preparing students for global academic and professional pathways.

Provisional Offer of Admission will be given to shortlisted applicants subject to confirmation based on the following:

  • Student visa endorsed to Amity University for joining full time Course. No other endorsement will be accepted. The visa should be valid for the prescribed duration of the course.
  • Submission of Medical Test Report within a week from the date of admission from a Registered Medical Practitioner OR a Medical Practitioner nominated by Amity University.
  • Adequate Medical Insurance Cover.
  • All Foreign students will be required to register themselves with the concerned District Foreigners Registration Officer / Foreigners Regional Registration Officers, within 14 days of your first arrival in India.

The following documents are required at the time of registration with the Foreigners Registration Officer:

  • Photocopy of Passport and visa
  • Four Photographs
  • Details of Residence in India
  • HIV / AIDS Test Report from one of the WHO recognized Institutions
  • Provisional Admission Letter from Amity University in support of your student visa.
