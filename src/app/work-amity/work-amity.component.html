Careers At Amity

Amity University Noida offers a wide range of career opportunities for individuals who aspire to contribute meaningfully to education, research, innovation, and institutional development. As one of India’s leading private universities, Amity provides a professionally enriching environment that values academic excellence, integrity, collaboration, and long-term growth.

Whether you are an experienced academician, a research-driven professional, or an individual seeking administrative or non-academic roles, a career at Amity University Noida allows you to be part of an institution committed to shaping future leaders and advancing knowledge across disciplines.

A Purpose-Driven Work Environment

Working at Amity University Noida means being part of a purpose-driven academic ecosystem that emphasizes teaching quality, research impact, and holistic institutional growth. The University fosters a culture where innovation, interdisciplinary thinking, and continuous learning are encouraged at every level.

Faculty members, researchers, and administrative professionals work collaboratively to support the University’s mission of delivering globally benchmarked education while addressing real-world challenges. Amity’s work environment promotes professional independence, academic freedom, and accountability, enabling individuals to contribute meaningfully within their respective roles.

Faculty and Research Career Opportunities

Amity University Noida offers diverse opportunities for faculty members and research professionals across a broad spectrum of disciplines. Faculty roles are available at various academic levels, including teaching-focused, research-oriented, and leadership positions within academic schools and departments.

Faculty members are encouraged to:

  • Engage in high-quality teaching and curriculum development
  • Pursue funded and independent research
  • Publish in reputed national and international journals
  • Collaborate across disciplines and institutions
  • Participate in conferences, workshops, and academic forums

The University provides a supportive research ecosystem with access to advanced laboratories, research centres, industry collaborations, and academic networks. Faculty members are also encouraged to contribute to policy, consultancy, and innovation-driven initiatives that strengthen the University’s academic standing.

Academic Administrative Position

In addition to teaching and research positions, Amity University Noida offers academic administrative and leadership roles for professionals with experience in academic planning, governance, student services, and institutional management.

These roles play a critical part in:

  • Academic coordination and programme administration
  • Curriculum implementation and quality assurance
  • Examination management and academic records
  • Faculty coordination and academic operations
  • Student engagement and academic support systems

Academic administrative positions provide opportunities to work closely with faculty leadership, academic councils, and institutional committees, contributing directly to the smooth functioning and strategic growth of the University.

Non-Academic and Professional Roles

Amity University Noida also offers a wide range of non-academic career opportunities across administrative, technical, and professional domains. These roles support the University’s operations and campus development, ensuring that academic and student-facing functions operate efficiently.

Non-academic roles may include opportunities in:

  • Administration and campus operations
  • Human resources and talent management
  • Finance and accounts
  • IT systems and digital infrastructure
  • Communications, branding, and outreach
  • Student services and support functions

Professionals in these roles contribute significantly to creating a structured, responsive, and student-centric campus environment.

Professional Growth and Development

Amity University Noida places strong emphasis on professional development and career progression. Employees are encouraged to upgrade their skills, pursue professional certifications, participate in training programmes, and engage in continuous learning initiatives.

The University supports growth through:

  • Faculty development programmes
  • Research workshops and training sessions
  • Leadership and management development initiatives
  • Exposure to academic and industry best practices
  • Opportunities for internal mobility and role enhancement

This focus on development ensures that individuals grow alongside the institution while building long-term careers at Amity.

Inclusive and Ethical Work Culture

Amity University Noida is committed to maintaining an inclusive, respectful, and ethical work environment. The University values diversity of thought, experience, and background, and strives to provide equal opportunities for professional advancement.

All roles at Amity are governed by transparent recruitment practices and clear performance expectations. The institution emphasizes professionalism, accountability, and adherence to ethical standards across academic and administrative functions.

How to Apply

Candidates interested in building a career at Amity University Noida can explore available opportunities under the relevant categories, including:

  • Faculty and Research Positions
  • Academic Administrative Positions
  • Non-Academic and Professional Roles

Applicants are advised to review role-specific requirements carefully and complete the online application process as outlined. Shortlisted candidates are contacted as per institutional recruitment procedures.

Be a Part of Amity’s Academic Journey

A career at Amity University Noida is more than a professional role; it is an opportunity to contribute to an institution that shapes minds, advances research, and builds future-ready leaders. By joining Amity, individuals become part of a dynamic academic community that values excellence, innovation, and long-term impact.
