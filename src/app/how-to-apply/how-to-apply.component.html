How to Apply

Applying to Amity University Noida is a fully online process. Candidates can complete registration, submit details, upload the required documents, and track progress through the admission portal.

Amity University Noida offers programmes across undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral levels. Admissions follow a structured online process, with programme-specific evaluation where applicable.

Steps to Apply
  • Start Your Application
     Click on “Apply Now” and select your applicant category, programme level, and discipline.
  • Register Online
     Enter your basic details and verify your mobile number and email ID through OTP to create your login credentials.
  • Pay the Application Fee
     Proceed with the payment through the available online options.
  • Complete the Application Form
     Fill in your personal, academic, and programme details. Review all entries carefully.
  • Upload Photograph
     Upload a recent passport-size photograph.
  • Review and Submit
     Check all details, submit the form, and track your application through the admission portal.
Application Fee and Payment

An application fee of ₹1500 is applicable for each submission. This fee is non-refundable.

Payment can be completed through standard online methods such as cards, net banking, or digital wallets.

Important Notes for Applicants
  • The process is entirely online.
  • A separate application is required for each programme.
  • Applicants should complete all steps within the given timelines.
  • Keep login credentials and payment details for future reference.

The application fee is non-refundable. Requests may be reviewed only in limited cases, such as duplicate transactions or verified technical issues during payment. Approval is subject to internal verification.

If you experience any technical difficulties while filling out or submitting the Amity University Noida application form, you are encouraged to report the issue using the support form provided below. This facility is available to assist candidates at every stage of the online admission process, including those who are attempting to apply online for university admission, complete their university application, or submit their admission application.

While completing the online admission form, applicants may occasionally face issues related to login access, form submission, document upload, payment confirmation, or application status updates. To ensure that your college application or admission process is not delayed, please share the required details accurately in the support form.

Information Required for Support

When reporting a technical issue, candidates are requested to provide:

  • Registered mobile number
  • Registered email address
  • A brief but clear description of the issue faced during the application form submission or university admissions process

Providing complete and correct information will help the support team quickly identify the issue and offer timely assistance.

How Support Is Provided

Once your request is submitted, the Amity admissions support team will review the details shared and contact you to help resolve the issue as efficiently as possible. This support applies to candidates who are looking to:

  • Apply to the Amity University Noida online
  • Complete their online admission form
  • Submit their application form
  • Track their admission application
  • Apply for courses or apply for degree programs at Amity University Noida

Applicants facing difficulties during the Amity University Noida online process are advised to raise concerns immediately to avoid missing important deadlines such as the Amity University Noida admission last date, Amity University Noida registration last date, or Amity University Noida last date to apply.

The University remains committed to ensuring a smooth and accessible university admissions experience for all applicants. Candidates are encouraged to use only the official admission portal and support channels while completing the Amity University application to ensure accurate resolution and timely assistance.
