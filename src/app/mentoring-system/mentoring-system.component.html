Student Mentoring System

Amity University Noida follows a well-defined Mentor–Mentee Concept as part of its student support framework. It is designed to support students throughout their academic journey. Under this framework, each faculty mentor is assigned to a small group of students, enabling regular academic interaction, guidance, and progress monitoring.

The mentoring system focuses on helping students navigate academic requirements, develop essential skills, and make informed decisions related to learning pathways and career planning. Faculty mentors play an important role in providing consistent support and early guidance, ensuring students remain engaged and confident during their time at the university.

In addition to faculty mentoring, Amity University Noida offers dedicated Student Counselling Services, where trained professionals provide guidance on personal, emotional, and professional challenges. These services work in coordination with the mentoring framework to ensure students have access to the right support systems when required.

Together, the mentoring and counselling structure creates a supportive academic environment that promotes student well-being, continuity in learning, and overall development. This integrated approach reflects Amity University Noida’s commitment to student support, academic success, and holistic growth.
