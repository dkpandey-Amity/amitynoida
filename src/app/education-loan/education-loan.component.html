Education Loan

Pursuing higher education is a significant investment, and financial support can play a crucial role in helping students achieve their academic goals. To make quality education accessible, Amity University Noida has partnered with several leading banks and financial institutions across India to support students who wish to apply for an education loan.

These partnerships are designed to simplify the education loan process and help students and parents understand how to get an education loan, whether they are planning to study in India or abroad. Through these tie-ups, Amity assists students in accessing reliable higher education loan options with faster processing and transparent procedures.

Education Loan Benefits for Amity Noida Students

Banks associated with Amity University Noida offer a range of advantages to students applying for a student education loan including:

  • Coverage for all courses offered at Amity University Noida, including UG, PG, and professional programmes
  • Loan coverage for tuition fees, books, hostel and boarding charges, and airfare expenses for overseas programmes, as applicable
  • Concessional interest rates as per prevailing education loan policy
  • Speedy processing of education loan applications to reduce delays in admission
  • Wide availability of loans across India, supporting students searching for an education loan near me
  • Guidance through the student loan process, making it easier to understand how to apply for an education loan or how to take an education loan after 12th
Understanding the Education Loan Process

Students who wish to get an education loan should familiarise themselves with the education loan procedure and eligibility requirements set by individual banks. While criteria may vary, banks generally assess academic background, admission confirmation, repayment capacity, and collateral (if applicable).

Whether you are asking can a student get an education loan, who is eligible for an education loan, or how much time it takes to get an education loan, Amity’s partner banks and loan assistants help guide applicants through the study loan application and education loan form submission stages.

These loan options are available for students seeking education loan for study in India, including those applying from different regions such as education loan in Delhi, education loan in Bihar, and other states.

Illustrative List of Documents Required for an Education Loan

To complete the education loan application form, students may be required to submit the following documents (requirements may vary by bank and loan scheme):

  • Admission letter from Amity University Noida, including year-wise fee structure, boarding and lodging costs, and scholarship confirmation if applicable (often required for the application for fee structure for education loan)
  • Attested copies of documents as proof of age/date of birth and residential address
  • Passport-sized photographs of the student applicant, co-obligants, and guarantors
  • Copies of mark sheets and degree certificates of previous academic qualifications
  • Income proof or latest Income Tax Returns of parents, co-obligants, or guarantors, if required
  • Details of collateral security, along with a valuation certificate from a government-approved valuer, if applicable
  • Bank account statements of the student, co-obligants, or guarantors for the last six months
  • Copy of passport or visa and documents related to airfare costs for students applying for overseas programmes
PM-Vidyalaxmi Scheme

The PM-Vidyalaxmi Scheme, a Government of India initiative offering collateral-free and guarantor-free education loans is available for all students who are getting admission in this Institution and who are desirous of availing education loans to pursue their higher education. You can visit the portal https://pmvidyalaxmi.co.in . The portal does not charge any amount from the applicant.

Through the portal, you can:

  • Apply to multiple banks for education loan.
  • Access collateral-free and guarantor-free education loans.
  • Benefit of interest subsidies available to eligible students as per scheme guidelines accessible at PM-Vidyalaxmi- A unified education loan application portal
  • To address queries raised by the students, there is a dedicated Toll-free number 1800-1031.
