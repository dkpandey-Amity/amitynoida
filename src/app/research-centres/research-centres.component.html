Research Centres

Amity University Noida has established 50 hi-end Research Centres and Research Labs across diverse domains of Applied Science, Technology, Management, and Liberal Arts. These Research Centres serve as advanced Research Facilities dedicated to academic research and university research, and are striving to commercially scale up innovations to find solutions to some of the most pressing issues affecting the world.
