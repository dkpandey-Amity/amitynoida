The academic ecosystem is designed to serve learners at different stages of their educational journey, from students exploring courses after 12th to those pursuing advanced degree programmes in India.

Amity University offers one of the most comprehensive college course lists available today, making it a preferred destination for students seeking quality higher education courses and university courses in Noida that are aligned with global academic and industry standards.

Academic Framework Aligned with Global Best Practices

The academic structure at Amity is designed to mirror the systems followed by some of the world’s leading universities. Course design, delivery, assessment, and review mechanisms are continuously benchmarked against international standards to ensure relevance and quality.

Dynamic Semester-Based Course Structure

All programmes follow a semester-based system, where each academic year is divided into two semesters of approximately 17 to 18 weeks each. This structure allows for:

  • Continuous engagement between faculty and students
  • Better pacing of learning outcomes
  • Deeper academic exploration through research assignments and term papers

The semester system is particularly beneficial for students enrolled in UG courses in Noida and PG courses in Noida, as it exposes them to a globally recognised academic model while maintaining strong academic discipline.

The curriculum integrates traditional academic foundations with emerging trends across disciplines. This ensures that students pursuing degree programmes in India, including engineering courses and management courses, are prepared to meet current and future industry requirements.

System of Education

Amity University follows a rigorous and transparent system of academic evaluation and governance to maintain consistency and excellence across all programmes.

Multi-Stage Review and Appraisal

Each programme undergoes a structured bi-annual review process involving:

  • The Area Expert Committee
  • The Board of Studies
  • The Academic Council

These bodies evaluate course relevance, academic depth, learning outcomes, and alignment with industry and statutory requirements. This ensures that all higher education courses remain updated, relevant, and academically sound.

Outcome-Based Course Evaluation

Course objectives and learning outcomes are regularly assessed based on:

  • Placement feedback and employability data
  • Performance of graduating students
  • Technological advancements and industry trends
  • Statutory and regulatory guidelines

This outcome-driven approach ensures that students enrolled in university courses in Noida graduate with skills and knowledge that are aligned with real-world expectations.

Introduction of Future-Focused Degree Programmes

To stay ahead of emerging global trends, Amity University continuously introduces future-oriented degree courses in advanced and interdisciplinary areas. These include programmes in:

  • Solar Energy
  • Nuclear Science and Technology
  • Space Science
  • Nanotechnology

Such offerings strengthen Amity’s position as an institution that not only provides traditional undergraduate courses in India and postgraduate courses in India, but also leads in developing programs that address future scientific and technological challenges.

Internal Evaluation System

Academic performance at Amity is assessed through a comprehensive internal and continuous evaluation system. Internal assessment carries a 30% weightage in each semester and includes clearly defined components such as:

  • Class tests and quizzes
  • Viva and presentations
  • Case discussions and seminars
  • Home assignments and projects
  • Term papers and attendance

This system promotes consistent learning and reduces over-dependence on end-term examinations. It is particularly effective for students enrolled in engineering courses, management courses, and other full-time degree courses, where practical understanding and application are essential.

Grade System and Academic Progression

Student performance is evaluated using a ten-point grading system, reflected through letter grades such as A+, A, A-, B+, B, B-, C+, C, and F. Each grade carries defined grade points and qualitative meaning.

Academic progress is measured through:

  • Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA)
  • Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)

Depending on batch size and programme structure, Amity University follows both absolute and relative grading systems. Students must maintain the prescribed SGPA and CGPA to:

  • Pass each semester
  • Progress to the next academic level
  • Qualify for the award of a degree or diploma

This structured evaluation framework ensures fairness, transparency, and academic rigour across all degree programmes in India offered by the University.

 

Attendance Policy

Amity University places strong emphasis on academic discipline and engagement. Students are required to maintain 100% attendance in lectures, tutorials, practicals, and seminars. In specific contingency cases, relaxation of up to 25% may be granted.

However, a minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory to be eligible to appear for examinations. This policy fosters regularity, accountability, and sustained academic growth, especially for students enrolled in UG courses in Noida, PG courses in Noida, and other professional programmes.

Continuous Curriculum Updation

Curriculum updation is a continuous and structured process at Amity University. Feedback from multiple sources, including placements, industry interactions, employability trends, technological advancements, and statutory bodies, is systematically reviewed.

The updation process follows a defined hierarchy:

  1. Review by subject and industry experts
  2. Evaluation by the Board of Studies
  3. Final approval by the Academic Council

This ensures that students pursuing courses after 12th, higher education courses, and advanced degree programs in India remain industry-ready and academically competitive.

Personalized Attention and Student Support

Mentor-Mentee System

Amity’s Mentor-Mentee concept ensures personalised academic and personal guidance. Each faculty member mentors a small group of students, providing support related to academics, career planning, and personal development.

This system is particularly valuable for students enrolled in engineering courses, management courses, and other university courses in Noida, helping them develop confidence, discipline, and professional skills.

Counselling and Wellbeing Support

Amity University also offers a strong counselling framework. Students facing academic, emotional, or personal challenges have access to trained counsellors from reputed institutions such as NIMHANS and professionals trained in the USA.

These services ensure that students feel supported and secure throughout their academic journey, enabling them to focus fully on learning and growth.
