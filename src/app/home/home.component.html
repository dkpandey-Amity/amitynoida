Welcome to Amity University Noida

As India’s top-ranked private, non-profit university, Amity University Noida is renowned for its academic rigour and UGC-recognized programs. In addition to being a premier private university in Noida, we are a pioneer in learning, innovation, and leadership, shaping the future of education.
