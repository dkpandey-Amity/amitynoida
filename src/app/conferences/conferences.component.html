Conferences

To create a forum for discussion, knowledge, and idea sharing, Amity University Noida has brought together intellectuals and luminaries from diverse fields ranging from science, technology, and commerce to the judiciary and management. These academic conferences enable experts to share their insights with Amity faculty and students, strengthening the culture of university conferences and research conferences.

Amity University Noida has facilitated interaction with over 1,500 corporate leaders and more than 50 Global Gurus through international conferences and management conferences. These engagements take place across platforms such as academic and research seminars, webinars, guest lectures, workshops, training sessions, and professional development programs, enriching learning through meaningful exchange.
