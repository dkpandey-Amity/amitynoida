Leadership

Dr. Ashok K. Chauhan, the Founder President of Amity Education Group, is a visionary leader, entrepreneur, philanthropist, and pioneer in educational reform. His three decades of successful entrepreneurship in Europe inspired him to lay the foundation with a clear vision: to empower talented youth with holistic, value-driven, and high-quality education, helping them evolve into well-rounded and successful individuals. Today, Amity is a leading global education group owing to his unwavering dedication that shapes India's educational landscape, inspiring generations to dream and achieve. His role continues to set benchmarks in higher education leadership and university leadership across the country.

Dr. Ashok K. Chauhan

(The Foundation of Amity Institutions and the sponsoring body of Amity Universities) Chairman, AKC Group of Companies

My mission is nation-building through education and beyond.

Each student of Amity will become a success story.

Amity is not just a group of institutions that imparts world-class education, but an experience where everyone associated is part of a dream, a mission to see that we nurture truly great leaders. This reflects his outstanding contribution to academic leadership in India.

In my over 30 years' stay in West Germany, I realised, through interacting with the over 6000 Europeans working for me, that we Indians have the intelligence, the talent and the potential to be the most successful people in the world. That is why we established the Ritnand Balved Education Foundation in 1986 to start institutions that will groom young future leaders to be complete, value-driven human beings and competent professionals with a deep passion for humanity. These will be the people who will lead the world.

Today, we are proud that Amity is synonymous with practical, industry-focused education and attracts the best students. The fact that our alumni are working in the best companies across the world is a testament to our extremely talented faculty who inculcate, by example, in each student the spirit of dedication, sincerity and loyalty, and to all the corporates who support our mission with so much enthusiasm. This commitment continues to strengthen Amity’s reputation in higher education administration.

Each one of our students will be a success story. This is my dream; this is my commitment.

Dr. Ashok K. Chauhan

Dr. Atul Chauhan

Dr. Atul Chauhan is the President of the not-for-profit Ritnand Balved Education Foundation and the Chancellor of Amity University. Dr. Chauhan is also the CEO of the AKC Group of Companies.

Dr. Chauhan is a member of many Government and Statutory Bodies, including being a distinguished Fellow of the Institute of Directors and a member of the Expert Committee of the University Grants Commission (UGC), a statutory body of the Government of India. His contributions highlight his prominent role in university administration and academic administration.

Dr. Chauhan sits on many boards, including Assocham Europe, Netherlands-India Financial Corridor and Malaysia-India Committee on Education.

Born and brought up in Germany, Dr. Chauhan had his higher education in engineering and finance from University College London and the London School of Economics, after which he took on the responsibility of heading the London Office.

Dr. Chauhan has received several awards from esteemed organisations like the Economic Times and News 24.

Prof. (Dr.) Balvinder Shukla is the Vice Chancellor, Amity University Noida. She has over 22 years of experience in organizations such as Shimnit Group, Interra Software, IIHT Systems and Amity Group of Institutions. As a Director at Interra Software, she established RMG and the training department and played a pivotal role in ISO certification for the company. As the Head of Corporate Planning and Head (North) at Shimnit Group, she was instrumental in increasing the business turnover by 300% in the first year and establishing a strong process-driven organization and a strong sales force.

In 1990, she moved back to India and opted for a corporate career with IIHT Systems Ltd., as the Regional Head. She established a strong network of 72 franchises and dealers. Due to her excellent performance, she assumed the role of Executive Director within three years and was responsible for business development and strategic business alliances.

Dr. Shukla’s core competencies include establishing new businesses, HR planning, recruitment and development aligned with business needs, and process establishment. To her credit, she has presented many papers in seminars and at conferences conducted at national and international levels. She has organized over 150 seminars, conferences and management development programs. Dr. Shukla is also the Chairperson of more than 20 committees within Amity and outside. Her contributions reflect deep expertise in higher education administration.

She is on the advisory board of many companies, is a member of several professional bodies and is closely associated with many CSR projects. Dr. Shukla is a strategist, institution builder, mentor and an inspirational leader and relationship builder, along with being an avid researcher, teacher, trainer and entrepreneurial leader. Her specialities include strategic management, resource-based strategy, business restructuring, CRM, automation of processes and systems, creating and scaling up new businesses, change management, growth strategies, organization turnaround and entrepreneurial leadership.

Prof. (Dr.) Gurinder Singh is the Group Vice Chancellor at Amity Universities, Director General of Amity Group of Institutions, and Vice Chairman of the Global Foundation for Learning and Excellence, with over 28 years' expertise in institutional building, teaching, consultancy, research and industry. His contributions have significantly advanced academic leadership across global campuses.

As the youngest Founder Pro Vice Chancellor of Amity University for two terms, Founder Director General of Amity International Business School and Founder CEO of the Association of International Business School, London, he has spearheaded numerous Amity campus establishments globally.

A distinguished scholar in International Business with a Doctorate and Postgraduate degree from the Indian Institute of Foreign Trade, Dr. Singh is a notable speaker at prestigious international forums and has received over 25 national and international awards. His consistent contributions reflect excellence in university leadership and higher education leadership.
