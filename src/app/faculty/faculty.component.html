81 Amity faculty are featured in the list of top 2% scientists in the world, published by researchers from Stanford University

Amity University Noida takes immense pride in announcing that 81 members of the Amity University faculty have been featured among the top 2% scientists in the world, as identified in a globally recognised list published by researchers from Stanford University. This prestigious list acknowledges researchers whose work has demonstrated exceptional impact through sustained contributions to research, innovation, and scholarly publications across disciplines.

This recognition is a significant milestone not only for the individuals named but also for the wider academic ecosystem at Amity. The presence of 81 faculty members in this elite global ranking reflects the strength, depth, and credibility of the Amity University faculty list, particularly the Amity University Noida faculty list, which represents a strong concentration of research-led educators and scholars.

Global Recognition of Research Excellence

The Stanford-led global ranking identifies scientists whose research output, citation impact, and academic influence place them among the most impactful scholars worldwide. Inclusion in this list signifies consistent contributions to advancing knowledge, addressing real-world challenges, and shaping future research directions.

For Amity University, this achievement reinforces its position as a research-driven institution where teaching and inquiry go hand in hand. The recognition of 81 faculty members underscores the University’s commitment to building a robust academic culture that values innovation, interdisciplinary research, and global relevance.

This achievement also places the Amity University faculty in a distinguished global context, standing alongside researchers from some of the world’s most renowned universities and research institutions.

The Amity University faculty list reflects a diverse and accomplished group of scholars, researchers, and educators drawn from leading institutions in India and across the world.   • Strong academic foundations across disciplines

The recognition of faculty members in the global top 2% list is a testament to the quality of mentorship, scholarship, and intellectual leadership that defines the Amity University Noida faculty list.
