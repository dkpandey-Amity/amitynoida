The journey of AHF began in the year 2001. Established as a registered NGO under the aegis of Amity - India’s leading education group with 150,000 students, 8 universities, 12 business schools, 150+ institutions and 10 overseas campuses, the foundation truly reflects the social responsibility consciousness and concerns of Amity Group of Institutions which form an integral part of the group’s philosophy.

Since then, AHF has focused on core areas of development like education, health and governance.

It has set up a chain of schools like Amitasha and Atulasha to provide quality education to the underprivileged children.

It has undertaken projects on women empowerment, rehabilitation of the physically disabled, HIV/AIDS awareness and other socially relevant issues, thus bringing about a change in the lives of many.

Amitasha - Nurturing the girl child

Amitasha was established in the year 2000 as an initiative of Amity Humanity Foundation with 35 girl students. The mission was to offer free education to the underprivileged girls of the society and thereby providing them a level playing ground to realise their potential.

Today, Amitasha runs 5 schools in Delhi NCR where over 1,000 girls in the age group of 5-18 get educated. The schools are run in the campus of Amity International Schools (AIS) and boast of the best infrastructure required to provide the ideal learning environment to the children. Starting from Nursery, these girls remain with Amitasha till class XII under the able guidance of professionally qualified teachers. Amitasha also provides free meals, books, uniforms and health care for these children. Students who display extraordinary potential and interest are encouraged to study further at the other Amity institutions. Apart from academics, the girls are also given vocational training so that, tomorrow they have the ability to become economically independent.

The first batch of Amitasha girls appeared for Class XII exams conducted by CBSE, as students of Amity International School in the academic year 2013-14 and have got admission in good colleges of Delhi University. Thereafter, three more batches of Amitasha Girls have cleared Class XII exams so for.
