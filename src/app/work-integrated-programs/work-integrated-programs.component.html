MBA (for Working Professionals)

MBA program for working professionals with 2+ years of experience who are looking to accelerate their careers or pivot into a new role or industry. This design blends academic rigor, leadership development, and hands-on exposure to emerging industries and technologies, all while being accessible to busy professionals.
Program Objectives

MBA (Working Professionals) is designed for professionals seeking to:

  • Move into leadership or C-suite roles without pausing career
  • Leap from Functional expertise into confident leadership roles
  • Stay ahead of the Curve

Duration: 24 months, 4 semesters

Credit Units 102 CUs

Study Mode Regular/ Hybrid (Weekend) 10am-5pm

Mode Non residential, Residential facility on chargeable basis

Intake July, 30 per batch

Eligibility Bachelor's Degree of any Statutory University with minimum 50% marks with minimum two years' Work Experience after graduation.

Selection Procedure Personal Interview

Category Non sponsored, scholarship/ Company discount upto 50%

Fee Structure Rs 4.14 L per sem, Guest House/ Hostel facility on chargeable basis
