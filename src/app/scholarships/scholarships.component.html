Martyr's Scholarship

Offered to the wards of deceased soldiers and paramilitary personnel in recognition of the services rendered to the nation. 100% scholarship is awarded based on scrutiny of the Scholarship Committee.

  • Candidate must meet the minimum academic eligibility of the respective programme and appear in the prescribed selection process
  • The qualifying act in which the deceased attained martyrdom must be in line of duty; documentary evidence required
  • Scholarship granted after qualifying admission and joining the University
  • Full fee paid initially; scholarship amount adjusted in next semester payment
  • Scholarship granted annually for Year 1; continuation subject to guidelines and scholarship policy

Continuation of Scholarship

Scholarships are renewed annually. To continue receiving your scholarship from Year 2 onwards, you must maintain the following academic standing.

100%

Top 5% of Programme

Maintain position within the top 5% of the merit list for your programme to continue the full scholarship.

50%

Top 10% of Programme

Maintain position within the top 10% of the merit list for your programme to continue the 50% scholarship.

25%

Top 15% of Programme

Maintain position within the top 15% of the merit list for your programme to continue the 25% scholarship.
