Top Global Ranking

The unwavering commitment towards imparting world-class education is the hallmark of Amity Education Group. As one of the top universities in India, Amity University Noida continues to strengthen its position in national and global university rankings. In fact, Amity’s flagship university has been ranked among the top 3% universities globally by QS & THE, the world's leading university rankings organisations. This reflects the strong Amity University ranking and its growing presence in the world university ranking ecosystem.

The academic excellence, research impact, and global standing of Amity University, Noida, are consistently evaluated and recognised by leading national and international ranking and assessment agencies. These rankings reflect the university’s sustained commitment to quality teaching, research-driven learning, innovation, industry engagement, and graduate outcomes across disciplines.

National Rankings and Performance

At the national level, Amity University Noida continues to demonstrate strong and well-rounded performance across key academic domains. In the National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) 2025, released by the Ministry of Education, Government of India, Amity University Uttar Pradesh achieved notable positions across multiple categories, underscoring its multidisciplinary strength:

  • 22nd in the University category, highlighting its overall academic and institutional performance
  • 18th in Pharmacy, reflecting excellence in pharmaceutical education, research, and innovation
  • 37th in Engineering, demonstrating strong outcomes in technical education and applied research
  • 49th in Management, indicating consistent performance in business education, leadership development, and industry relevance

These rankings reaffirm the university’s standing among India’s leading higher education institutions and its ability to deliver quality education across diverse fields.

Global Rankings and International Recognition

On the global stage, Amity University Noida continues to strengthen its international presence and reputation. In the QS World University Rankings 2026, the university was placed in the 951–1000 band, reflecting its growing global footprint, international collaborations, and academic reputation. Additionally, it secured a position of =187 in the QS Asia University Rankings 2026, highlighting its competitiveness among leading universities across the Asian region.

Based on its overall performance in global ranking tables published by Times Higher Education (THE) and QS, Amity University Noida is listed among the top 3% of universities worldwide. This distinction reflects consistent progress across key global metrics such as teaching quality, research output, international outlook, knowledge transfer, and academic influence.

Subject-Wise Excellence: Times Higher Education Rankings

Amity University Noida has also achieved exceptional recognition in subject-specific rankings. In the Times Higher Education Subject Rankings 2025, the university secured leading positions within India across several disciplines:

  • 1st in Business and Economics, affirming leadership in management education and economic research
  • 1st among private universities in Computer Science, highlighting excellence in computing, emerging technologies, and innovation
  • 5th in Engineering, reflecting strong research performance and industry-aligned education
  • 2nd in Other Health Disciplines, showcasing strengths in health sciences and interdisciplinary healthcare education
  • 4th in Social Sciences, demonstrating impact in policy, governance, and social research
  • 4th in Physical Sciences, indicating robust contributions to scientific research and advanced laboratories

These rankings reinforce the university’s depth of expertise and its ability to perform at the highest level across multiple academic domains.

QS Subject Rankings and Discipline Leadership

In the QS World University Rankings by Subject 2025, Amity University Noida further strengthened its national leadership:

  • 1st in India for Hospitality and Leisure Management, reflecting excellence in curriculum design, industry engagement, and global relevance
  • 7th in India for Pharmacy and Pharmacology, underscoring strong research output and academic reputation
  • 1st among private universities in India for Agriculture and Forestry, highlighting its contribution to sustainable practices, agricultural innovation, and applied research

These subject rankings demonstrate the university’s focused investment in specialised disciplines and its ability to achieve national leadership in select academic areas.
