International Linkages

Global Partnerships That Shape Global Professionals

Amity University Noida has established one of India’s most extensive international academic networks, collaborating with over 100 leading universities and research institutions worldwide. Our tie-ups span prestigious institutions such as the University of Melbourne, Monash University, TU Delft, University of Birmingham, University of Southampton, University of Sheffield, Queen Mary University of London, Arizona State University, University College Dublin, George Washington University, American University, Illinois Tech, and James Cook University, among many others. These partnerships anchor Amity University Noida’s commitment to global education and future-ready learning.

Through these collaborations, students gain access to international classrooms, global research ecosystems, and multicultural academic exposure. Semester exchanges, short-term mobility programs, dual pathways, and visiting faculty engagements ensure that learners experience the best of global education without barriers. Many of Amity University Noida’s collaborative research initiatives, conducted with partner universities across Europe, North America, Asia, and Australia, lead to joint publications, innovation projects, and interdisciplinary breakthroughs.

How Students Benefit from Amity University Noida’s Global Network

Amity’s international linkages create tangible, career-shaping outcomes:

  • Study Abroad & Exchange Programs: Students spend a semester or summer at leading global universities.
  • Joint Research & Innovation: Access to shared labs, cross-border research teams, and high-impact global publications.
  • Dual & Twinning Programs: Begin studies at Amity and complete them at a partner university overseas.
  • Global Internships & Industry Exposure: Opportunities for multinational project work and international industry engagement.
  • Cross-Cultural Learning: Exposure to global perspectives that enhance professional readiness and employability.

These collaborations ensure that every student graduates with a global outlook, an internationally benchmarked skillset, and a competitive edge in the global job market. Amity University Noida continues to strengthen these partnerships to provide students with pathways that connect them to the world’s best opportunities.
