Academic Withdrawal Refund Guidelines 2026

The Application for Withdrawal can be duly filled on Amizone (www.amizone.net) Thereafter, a physical copy of the completed withdrawal application must be submitted to the University for processing (address shall be provided online in Amizone once the withdrawal form is completed). No other mode of request will be considered except above. Withdrawal application will be processed as per following AUUP refund Guidelines.

Once a withdrawal request is submitted through Amizone, it will be reviewed and processed strictly in accordance with the Amity University Undergraduate and Postgraduate (AUUP) Fee Refund Policy, as outlined below. Students are advised to read the policy carefully to understand timelines, refund percentages, and applicable deductions.

Refund Policy for Hostel Fees - 2026
  • If a student withdraws from hostel up to 45 days before the commencement of the respective programme, the student will receive a refund of 100% of the deposited hostel fees and the Security Deposit. However, an amount up to 5% of the hostel fees subject to a maximum of Rs.5000/- shall be deducted as processing charges.
  • If a student withdraws from hostel up to 30 days before the commencement of the respective programme, the student will receive a refund of 80% of the deposited hostel fees and the Security Deposit.
  • If a student withdraws from hostel up to 15 days before the commencement of the respective programme, the student will receive a refund of 60% of the deposited hostel fees and the Security Deposit.
  • If a student withdraws from hostel post the commencement of the respective programme, none of the hostel fees deposited will be refunded except for the Security Deposit.

Note -

  • Hostel fees paid towards admission after the commencement of the programme are strictly NON-REFUNDABLE.
  • Application for withdrawal from the Hostel is to be submitted online through Amizone (www.amizone.net). No other mode of request will be considered.
Admission Withdrawal Timeline and Refund Structure

The amount of fee refunded to a student depends on when the withdrawal request is submitted in relation to the start date of the academic session. The policy is applicable to all students seeking a college fee refund, irrespective of programme or level of study.

1. Withdrawal Up to 15 Days Before the Start of the Academic Session

If a student withdraws their admission up to 15 days before the commencement of the academic session, the University will refund:

  • 100% of the semester tuition fees, and
  • 100% of the Security Deposit,

after deducting processing charges of up to 5% of the fees, subject to a maximum deduction of ₹5,000.

This category is intended for students who decide not to proceed with their admission well in advance of the academic session.

2. Withdrawal Less Than 15 Days Before the Start of the Academic Session

If the withdrawal request is submitted less than 15 days before the start of the academic session, the student will be eligible for:

  • 90% refund of the semester tuition fees, and
  • 100% refund of the Security Deposit.

The University retains the remaining 10% of the semester fees to cover administrative and academic processing costs already incurred.

3. Withdrawal Up to 15 Days After the Start of the Academic Session

If a student withdraws within 15 days after the academic session has begun, the refund entitlement will be:

  • 80% refund of the semester tuition fees, and
  • 100% refund of the Security Deposit.

This period includes students who attend classes or orientation briefly but decide not to continue with the programme.

4. Withdrawal Between 16 and 30 Days After the Start of the Academic Session

If the withdrawal occurs between the 16th and 30th day after the start of the academic session, the student will receive:

  • 50% refund of the semester tuition fees, and
  • 100% refund of the Security Deposit.

This reduced refund reflects the increased academic and institutional resources utilised during this period.

5. Withdrawal On or After 30 Days from the Start of the Academic Session

If a student withdraws on or after 30 days from the commencement of the academic session, the policy states that:

  • No portion of the semester tuition fees will be refunded,
  • Only the Security Deposit will be refunded, subject to adjustment of dues.

This rule applies to all students, regardless of programme or category, seeking any form of college fees refund after this period.

Security Deposit Refund and Adjustment of Dues

The Security Deposit, wherever refundable under the timelines above, will be released only after adjusting any outstanding dues, if applicable. These may include, but are not limited to:

  • Alumni Life Membership Fee – ₹1,000 (Compulsory)
  • IT Damage Charges, if any
  • Administrative Damage Charges, if any
  • Central Library Charges
  • Academic-related dues, including:
    • Laboratory usage charges
    • Departmental library or book bank dues
    • CRC or other academic facility dues
  • Examination-related dues
  • ID Card charges of ₹500, if the ID card is not returned
  • Any other pending dues, such as:
    • Gate Pass charges
    • Duplicate ID card charges

Only after these amounts are fully adjusted will the remaining Security Deposit (if any) be refunded to the student.

Scholarship Withdrawal Cases

In cases where a student who has been offered an Amity scholarship decides to withdraw from the programme:

  • The student will be treated as a non-scholarship student for the purpose of refund calculation.
  • The standard Amity University fee refund guidelines described above will apply.
  • No special refund privileges or relaxations are granted due to scholarship status.
Important Notes for Students and Parents
  • All withdrawal and refund requests must be submitted only through Amizone.
  • Refunds are processed strictly as per University policy, with no exceptions outside the defined timelines.
  • Processing time for refunds may vary depending on verification and clearance of dues.
  • Students are advised to retain acknowledgement or reference numbers generated on Amizone for future communication.
  • The University reserves the right to modify or interpret the policy in accordance with regulatory requirements.

This policy ensures transparency, consistency, and fairness in handling admission withdrawals and fee refunds while balancing administrative and academic commitments of the University.
