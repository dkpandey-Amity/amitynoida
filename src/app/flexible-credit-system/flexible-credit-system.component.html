Flexible Credit System

Amity University Noida follows a Flexible Credit System that enables students to design their academic journey in line with their aptitude, academic interests, and career goals, while meeting prescribed program requirements. This system supports academic flexibility, interdisciplinary learning, and outcome-based education within a structured framework.

Under this system, students are required to complete a defined number of credit units, consisting of a combination of core courses and elective courses. Core courses establish strong foundational knowledge, while electives allow students to customise their learning experience.

Structure of the Flexible Credit System

The Flexible Credit System at Amity University Noida includes the following components:

  • Core Courses
     Mandatory courses that provide foundational and discipline-specific knowledge essential to the programme.
  • Specialisation Electives (SE)
     Courses aligned with a student’s chosen area of specialisation, enabling deeper subject expertise and advanced learning.
  • Domain Electives (DE)
     Elective courses offered within the broader academic domain, allowing students to expand contextual and applied understanding beyond their core specialisation.
  • Open Electives (OE)
     Courses offered by departments or institutions outside the student’s primary domain, promoting interdisciplinary exposure and academic breadth.
  • Non-Teaching Credit Courses (NTCC)
     Experiential and research-oriented components such as independent research studies, seminars, term papers, and minor projects, designed to strengthen research aptitude and analytical skills.
  • Value Addition Courses (VAC)
     Courses focused on behavioural sciences, business communication, foreign languages, and professional development, supporting employability and holistic growth.
Academic Outcomes

The Flexible Credit System supports:

  • Customised learning pathways
  • Interdisciplinary education
  • Research-oriented learning
  • Skill development aligned with industry needs

By offering a structured flexibility within a regulated academic framework, Amity University Noida ensures that students graduate with discipline-specific expertise, interdisciplinary awareness, and practical competencies required for academic progression and professional success.
