Patents Granted

Innovation is a core pillar of academic excellence and societal contribution at Amity University Noida. The Amity Advantage is the university’s strong performance in university patents, which reflects a long-term commitment to research-driven innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the translation of knowledge into solutions that address real-world challenges. With a rapidly growing portfolio of academic patents, Amity University Noida has emerged as a significant contributor to India’s intellectual property ecosystem.

The Amity team of faculty members, scientists, and researchers has filed over 2,600 research patents across a wide spectrum of disciplines, including Nanotechnology, Biotechnology, Biosensors, Forensic Science, Pharmaceuticals, Microbiology, Software, Information Technology, Herbals, Photovoltaics, Electronics, and Food Processing. These extensive patent filings demonstrate not only the scale of innovation at Amity but also the diversity and relevance of its research output.

This strong focus on research patents highlights the University’s ability to generate original knowledge, develop novel technologies, and create solutions with tangible societal and industrial impact. The growing number of university patents is a testament to the robust research culture fostered at Amity University Noida.

Here, education is designed to inspire innovation that goes beyond academic boundaries and addresses critical global challenges. Research is oriented toward sustainability, affordability, and social relevance, ensuring that innovations contribute meaningfully to humanity while supporting long-term development goals. Faculty members, research scholars, and students actively collaborate across disciplines to develop path-breaking ideas that result in impactful academic patents.

The University encourages researchers to think beyond publication and focus on intellectual property creation. This emphasis on patent filings ensures that novel ideas are protected, refined, and positioned for real-world application. By integrating innovation into teaching and research, Amity has created an ecosystem where creativity, problem-solving, and entrepreneurship thrive side by side.

Through sustained investment in research infrastructure, laboratories, and intellectual property support systems, Amity University Noida enables researchers to move seamlessly from concept development to patent grants. This structured approach strengthens the University’s position as a leader in university patents and reinforces its role in shaping the future of innovation-led education.

AMITY UNIVERSITY COMMERCIALISING TECHNOLOGY TO USHER IN SOCIAL CHANGE

Amity’s guiding philosophy for research and innovation is centred on delivering affordable, scalable, and impactful solutions to real-world problems. The University believes that the true value of research patents lies not only in their novelty but also in their ability to create positive social change. Every innovation is developed with the intent to move beyond laboratories and enter mainstream application through commercialisation.

Several Amity innovations exemplify this approach. The development of the magic fungus “ROOTONIC,” known for enhancing crop productivity and soil health, demonstrates how academic patents can support sustainable agriculture. Similarly, a low-cost water purifier that operates without an external power supply addresses the urgent need for safe drinking water in resource-constrained regions.

Other notable innovations include an iodine-based fingerprint powder capable of developing latent fingerprints long after a crime has occurred, contributing significantly to forensic science and law enforcement. A low-cost LPG sensor designed to enhance household safety further reflects Amity’s commitment to socially relevant innovation. These technologies illustrate how patent grants are translated into practical solutions that improve quality of life.

By focusing on commercialisation, Amity University Noida ensures that university patents do not remain confined to academic records. Instead, they evolve into technologies, products, and services that benefit society at large. This approach strengthens the link between patent filings, industry engagement, and societal impact.

PATENTS AND COPYRIGHTS

Amity University Noida’s continued emphasis on research, innovation, and intellectual property has resulted in more than 2,600 patent filings, reinforcing its strong position in the domain of academic patents and research patents. These filings reflect a consistent and systematic effort to protect innovations emerging from teaching, research, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Of these filings, over 450 patents have been granted, underscoring the quality, originality, and applicability of Amity’s research output. Each patent grant represents a rigorous evaluation process and validates the novelty and usefulness of the innovation. This success rate highlights the maturity of Amity’s research ecosystem and its alignment with global intellectual property standards.

The University actively supports researchers throughout the intellectual property lifecycle—from ideation and documentation to patent filings, prosecution, and eventual patent grants. Dedicated support structures ensure that faculty members, scholars, and students are well-equipped to navigate the complexities of intellectual property protection.

Through its growing portfolio of university patents, Amity University Noida continues to strengthen its role as a hub of innovation, knowledge creation, and societal transformation. The sustained focus on academic patents and research patents reflects the University’s vision of leading innovation-driven education while contributing meaningfully to national and global development.

Together, these achievements reaffirm Amity University Noida’s position as an institution where research excellence translates into protected innovation, impactful technologies, and long-term value for society.
