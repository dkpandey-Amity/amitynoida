Amity University Logo

The Amity University Noida logo is represented in a shield divided into dark blue and golden yellow sections by an upward sweeping line that symbolises continuous growth and progress achievable through education. While the shield conveys protection, integrity, and the resolute shelter of truth, the two contrasting colours highlight a unique blend of modernity and tradition, which forms the foundation of Amity’s philosophy.

The dark blue colour signifies the qualities of technology, performance, discipline, and academic advancement that define Amity University’s forward-looking approach to higher education. In contrast, the golden yellow colour reflects warmth, culture, values, and the timeless heritage that guides the institution’s character. Together, these colours create a powerful visual identity that embodies balance, purpose, and excellence.

At the heart of the Amity University emblem lies a radiant flame that stands for purity, enlightenment, and a passion for knowledge. The flame’s upper and lower arcs subtly reinterpret the shapes of the letters ‘A’ and ‘U’, representing Amity University. This thoughtful design reinforces brand recall while embedding meaning into every visual element.

As a globally recognised academic institution, the Amity University logo serves not just as a symbol but also as a commitment, representing the university’s mission to create empowered, ethically grounded, and future-ready leaders. The crest reflects Amity’s dedication to innovation, academic quality, and the holistic development of every student, making it an enduring icon of trust and educational excellence.
