Core Functions and Responsibilities
Placement and Internship Services

Amity University Noida's CRC places immense emphasis on securing suitable campus placements and internships for students upon successful completion of their programs. It works to ensure every student develops the right professional skills, domain knowledge, and industry exposure to become acceptable to industry partners. Strong placement support, along with structured career counselling, helps students prepare for their future careers.

Corporate-Institute Interface Management

The CRC is responsible for managing the corporate-institute interface. The Centre is actively involved in university brand-building exercises while facilitating seamless connections between students and corporate leaders. This ongoing career support enables meaningful engagement between academia and industry.

Skill Development and Industry Readiness

Beyond its core placement functions, the CRC is committed to continual improvement in student activities through various support functions. Workshops and activities are organised in the departments to strengthen skill development and help students become effective team leaders and collaborative team players with strong professional skills.

Strategic Approach

The CRC operates with a dual focus:

Internal Competency Development:
Fostering contemporary grooming and professional development of students through continuous career guidance

Industry Alignment:
Enabling industry partners to identify and recruit intellectuals with the required technical and managerial skills

Meeting Modern Industry Expectations

Changing business paradigms in today’s world demand that academic institutions foster a new breed of professionals endowed with:

  • Comprehensive knowledge and technical ability
  • Out-of-the-box thinking capabilities
  • Innovation and adaptability
  • Problem-solving skills for complex decision-making

Industry expectations have changed drastically. Hard work alone cannot guarantee success anymore, but smart work, consistency, and an attitude of embracing challenges at the right time can help individuals climb the performance ladder daily.

Professional Development at Amity University Noida

The primary purpose of the CRC is the creation of knowledge, enhancement of engineering and professional skills, and global integration of talent. Amity University Noida provides a highly professional learning environment to each student from day one, supported by continuous career counselling and career support.

The university emphasises:

  • Inculcating corporate values and professional ethics
  • Developing complex decision-making capabilities
  • Building superior expertise in functional domains
  • Gaining thorough business knowledge
CRC Initiatives and Programs

The Career Resource Centre conducts various initiatives throughout students' academic journeys to strengthen skill development, career guidance, and placement support:

  • Industry Expert Talks: Bringing real-world insights into the classroom
  • Skill Development Workshops: To enhance technical and soft skills
  • Industrial Projects: Hands-on experience with real-life business challenges
  • Operational Training: Building practical competencies for workplace readiness
  • Career Guidance: Customised mentoring for long-term career planning
Continuous Support System

The CRC at Amity University Noida provides ongoing career support beyond graduation, ensuring students remain connected with industry trends and opportunities. This comprehensive approach prepares students not just for their first job through campus placements, but for long-term career success in dynamic global markets.
