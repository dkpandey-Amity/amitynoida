Sangathan

Inter-Amity Institutions Sports Meet

The Inter-Amity Institutions Sports Meet, popularly known as Sangathan, is one of the most anticipated and celebrated sporting events in the Amity education ecosystem. Held annually, this month-long mega sports festival brings together students from Amity University Noida campuses and Amity institutions across India, creating a powerful platform for competitive sport, camaraderie, and institutional unity.

Sangathan is more than just a tournament; it is a celebration of physical excellence, teamwork, discipline, and the indomitable spirit of sport. As a flagship university sports meet, it reflects Amity’s belief that sports and physical fitness are essential components of holistic education and character building.

A Nationwide University Sports Festival

Students from multiple Amity institutions converge to participate in Sangathan, making it a truly large-scale inter-university sports and college sports event. The festival features a comprehensive range of sporting disciplines, allowing athletes with diverse strengths to compete at a high level. Events typically include athletics, football, cricket, basketball, volleyball, badminton, table tennis, chess, lawn tennis, kabaddi, and other team and individual sports.

This diversity ensures broad participation and inclusivity, enabling students from different academic backgrounds to showcase their sporting talent. The competitive format promotes fairness, professionalism, and respect for sporting rules, mirroring the standards of national-level competitions.

Promoting Teamwork, Discipline, and Leadership

Sangathan plays a vital role in fostering core values such as teamwork, perseverance, leadership, and discipline. Students represent their respective institutions with pride, learning the importance of collaboration, strategic thinking, and resilience under pressure. The experience of training, competing, and supporting teammates strengthens interpersonal bonds and builds a strong sense of belonging within the Amity community.

Participation in this annual sports meet also helps students develop mental toughness, time management skills, and sportsmanship, qualities that translate seamlessly into academic and professional success.

Founder’s Day and the Spirit of Excellence

One of the most distinctive aspects of Sangathan is its culmination with Founder’s Day celebrations, observed in honour of excellence in education, leadership, and sports. This convergence symbolises Amity’s integrated vision of intellectual growth and physical development. Outstanding athletes and teams are recognised for their achievements, reinforcing a culture that values effort, discipline, and excellence in every domain.

The Founder’s Day celebrations add a ceremonial and inspirational dimension to the sports meet, making Sangathan not just a competition, but a meaningful institutional tradition.

Building a Strong Sports Culture

At Amity University, Noida, sports are viewed as a critical pillar of student development. Sangathan strengthens this philosophy by providing students with exposure to high-quality competition, professional event organisation, and large-scale sporting infrastructure.

The event also encourages participation beyond athletes; students contribute as organisers, coordinators, volunteers, and supporters. This involvement enhances leadership skills, event management experience, and teamwork, further enriching the overall campus experience.

A Celebrated Annual Tradition

Over the years, Sangathan has evolved into a hallmark university sports festival and a much-awaited highlight of the academic calendar. Its scale, energy, and inclusive spirit set it apart as a premier inter-university sports meet, reinforcing Amity’s commitment to nurturing well-rounded individuals.

By blending competitive sport with celebration and community spirit, the Inter-Amity Institutions Sports Meet continues to inspire students to strive for excellence, on the field, in the classroom, and in life.



A perfect mélange of over 150 Amity institutions, 17 Amity schools & pre-schools from 15 cities of India as well as students from Amity London, Singapore, New York, California, Dubai & Mauritius campus come to Amity grounds to pay their regards and respect to their Founder President. The unfurling of “Sangathan Flag” marks the beginning of the glittering ceremony followed by an impressive march past by over 5000 students of Amity.

Students compete in 35 different sports and games including badminton, squash, basketball, volleyball, karate, judo, cricket, chess, swimming, tug of war to clinch the much coveted trophies.

The students of various institutions also present a colourful cultural programme depicting the different cultures followed in different states of India.
