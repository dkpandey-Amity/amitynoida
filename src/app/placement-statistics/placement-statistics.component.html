Future-Ready Education Career-Defining Outcomes

At Amity University Noida, career readiness is not an afterthought. It is built into the fabric of every programme. The Career Resource Centre (CRC) works year-round to connect students with leading organisations across industries, from global technology giants and top-tier consulting firms to hospitals, law firms, banks, and Fortune 500 companies.

Recognised by Times Higher Education for graduate employability outcomes for five consecutive years, Amity University Noida stands among a select group of Indian institutions acknowledged globally for the career readiness of its graduates.
